A LOVE LETTER TO NIHA

From Us to You.

We made this little corner of the universe for you, Niha. A place for our words, our memories, and all the love that still has nowhere to go but towards you. We miss you. We miss you so much.

✦

Eesha

My dearest Nihahaha, my wifu, my beloved, I sat outside all night waiting for the morning to come, and then it did. It was cold and so loudly quiet. I know thousands of miles away they were putting you to the ground, and its awful and its morbid but that’s what I was thinking. The sun took its time to come out that morning because it had to borrow the energy to bring light into the world after you were gone. I sat and I sat and I tried to understand what it meant that you’re gone, but in my lonely, cold, quiet balcony, I couldn’t come to terms with it. How could I? I never got to say my final goodbye to you. But, maybe that’s a good thing, maybe that’s means this is just a break and that maybe we’ll see each other soon and I hope you laugh at me for all the crying I’ve been doing alone and then you tell me, ‘’cheeks pull karlungi’’ My light, my sunshine, this is probably my coping mechanism, a way to keep sending words to you in hopes the heavens have the Wi-Fi. I know I used to say we all will go our different ways, and that’s heartbreaking and painful and inevitable, but it meant we would have houses in different countries. Well, make a pretty house in heavens, you got there first, and cut fruits, we’ll visit you soon, we’ll have a feast again, we’ll cut birthday cakes again, we’ll laugh again, we’ll dance again, and I'm not sure if Himesh's songs would be allowed, but we'll make music with our bare hands, and if not, we'll dance to the sound of our laughter. And we’ll never say goodbye again.

With love, Eesha

Zainab

Dearest nihahahahaha, We first met at 16. Two dumb kids in a new place, trying to find our place. You found yours on the first bench, I found mine on the last. But even then, talking to you was the easiest thing in the world. I remember, we would meet in the bathroom and you would fix your perfectly straight hair and i would wonder how it feels to have perfect hair. But I never asked. When we both went to Habib, I think I clung onto you for dear life. You were one of the very few people I knew, and god forbid, I have to spend time around strangers. So we stuck together and that was that. We both made different friends, we both spent our days with different people, but somewhere during the day we would meet and it felt like coming back home. You were the friend I could imitate himesh with, you were the friend who played candy crush with me in class, you were the friend who would fill my gallery with the weirdest selfies ever, you were the friend I would do photoshoots with in a literal bathroom. You were always there, and I was counting on you to keep doing that for a long long time. You were always so smart and funny and strong. I wish you weren't so strong. At the end of every day, it was just you and me in a car. Recapping everything. Shitting on everyone. Laughing at everyone. It was our little time and our little secret and no one else. I know you didn't always speak your mind, but I like to think I made you. In the same cars, every day. And I felt so special every time you told me something about yourself I couldn't tell anyone else. It was like I was back in 5th grade. So I kept so many secrets and I never said a word, and I feigned surprise when you told everyone. I never had trouble keeping your secrets. Until the last one. It was the heaviest secret you asked me to keep. And I couldn't. It weighed more than the weight of the world. I was Atlas, but only, I couldn't shoulder it. So I didn't keep the last secret, Niha. And I'm so sorry I never got to tell you. I never got to scold you for it. I never got to see you after the day I couldn't shut up about coconut oil. I know we're not supposed to have favourites. But you and I were always a team. You and I could get through anything. We were just together, everywhere. And I take so much pride in the fact that you opened up to me when you were vulnerable and we got to share so much, so many memories, with everyone and on our own. So many places we went to, so many playlists we listened to, so many different groups we belonged to, so many moments that were only ours. I am so mad at you for leaving. You promised you wouldn't. You gave me hope for so long. Until you didn't. But I love you and I know you were stronger than you needed to be and I know you will watch over us and laugh at how stupid I look going back home alone in a car, without you.

With love, Zainab
✧

And from all of us, always.

Eesha · Mahnoor · Rida · Zainab
