WHY WE MADE THIS

For the words that stay.

This website is our little way of keeping a space for Niha in the world. A place where the people who love her can come back, remember her, talk to her, and leave the words they are still carrying.

We chose the stars because they remind us that love can reach beyond what we can see or hold. Every letter left here becomes a little light in her sky. Every star holds someone's memory, a thought, a story, or a message meant for her.

This is not a goodbye. It is a place for missing her, loving her, remembering her, and making sure there is always somewhere to find her name.

This little universe is for you, Niha. Always.
