A LOVE LETTER TO NIHA

From Us to You.

We made this little corner of the universe for you, Niha. A place for our words, our memories, and all the love that still has nowhere to go but towards you. We miss you. We miss you so much.

✦

Eesha

My dearest Nihahaha, my wifu, my beloved, I sat outside all night waiting for the morning to come, and then it did. It was cold and so loudly quiet. I know thousands of miles away they were putting you to the ground, and its awful and its morbid but that’s I was thinking. The sun took its time to come out because it had to borrow the energy to bring light into the world after you were gone. I sat and I sat and I tried to understand what it meant that you’re gone, but in my lonely, cold, quiet balcony, I couldn’t come to terms with it. How could I? I never got to say my final goodbye to you. Maybe that’s a good thing, maybe that’s meant this is just a break and that maybe we’ll see each other soon and I hope you laugh at me for all the crying I’ve been doing alone and then you tell me, ‘’cheeks pull karlungi’’ My light, my sunshine, this is probably my coping mechanism, a way to keep sending words to you in hopes the heavens have the Wi-Fi. I know I used to say we all will go our different ways, and that’s heartbreaking and painful, but it meant we would have houses in different countries. Well, make a pretty house in heavens and cut fruits, we’ll visit you soon, we’ll have a feast again, we’ll cut birthday cakes again, we’ll laugh again, we’ll dance again. And we’ll never say goodbye again.

With love, Eesha
